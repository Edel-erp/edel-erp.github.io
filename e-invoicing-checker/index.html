Questions

Does e-invoicing apply to my business?

The Ministry of Revenues is rolling out electronic invoicing in phases, starting with larger and VAT-registered taxpayers. Others follow in later phases. Use the checker above, and confirm your phase with the Ministry of Revenues.

What do I need to prepare?

Your TIN certificate and trade licence, the addresses of your branches, and your list of items and prices. If you use a cash register or software, its reports make setup faster.

Can I keep my current cash register or receipt books?

Registered e-invoices must come from an approved system. Edel runs in a web browser on the computers, tablets and phones you already have, and can print on your existing receipt printer.

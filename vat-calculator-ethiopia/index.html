Questions

How do I calculate 15% VAT in Ethiopia?

Multiply the price before VAT by 0.15 to get the VAT, then add it to the price. For example, ETB 2,000 + 15% VAT (ETB 300) = ETB 2,300.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the total by 1.15 to get the price before VAT. The difference is the VAT. For example, ETB 1,150 ÷ 1.15 = ETB 1,000, so the VAT is ETB 150.

Does every business charge VAT?

VAT-registered businesses charge 15% VAT. Some goods and services are exempt, and businesses that are not VAT-registered pay turnover tax instead. Check your situation with the Ministry of Revenues.

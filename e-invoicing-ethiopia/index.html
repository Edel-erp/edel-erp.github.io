What is e-invoicing in Ethiopia?

With electronic invoicing, each sale is sent to the Ministry of Revenues system as it happens.

The invoice comes back with an official number and a QR code. Anyone can scan that code to check the invoice is genuine.

For you, it means every sale is on record the moment it happens. For your customer, it means proof that the invoice is real.

What changes at the till

Only one step changes. Scanning items, taking cash or a transfer and printing the receipt stay the same.

  • Before the receipt is handed over, the sale is sent to the Ministry and registered.
  • Your customer sees an official invoice number and a QR code on every receipt.
  • Refunds and corrections are done with a credit note, which is registered too.

One tip: train your cashiers on refunds and credit notes before you start. Corrections are where most early mistakes happen.

Who has to switch, and when

The Ministry of Revenues is rolling out electronic invoicing in phases, starting with larger and VAT-registered taxpayers.

Not sure where your business stands? Answer four questions and we'll tell you what e-invoicing likely means for you.

What you need to prepare

Getting ready is mostly about having your details in order. Before you start, gather:

  1. Your TIN certificate and trade licence.
  2. A list of your branches with their addresses.
  3. A clean item list with prices, and which items carry VAT at 15%.
  4. Which device each branch will use: the computer, tablet or phone you already have.
  5. Who will issue invoices, and who is allowed to correct them.

Questions

Do I need to buy a new machine?

No. Edel runs in a web browser on the computer, tablet or phone you already have.

Can I keep my receipt printer?

Yes. You can connect your existing receipt printer and keep printing receipts for your customers.

What happens when a customer returns something?

You issue a credit note. It is registered like an invoice, so the refund is on record and your sales stay correct.

Can I issue invoices in Amharic?

Edel works in Amharic and English, and invoices can show their labels in both languages.

What does the QR code do?

Anyone can scan the QR code on the invoice to check that it was registered with the Ministry of Revenues. It is your customer's proof that the invoice is genuine.

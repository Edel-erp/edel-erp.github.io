What Edel does for supermarkets and retail

A supermarket lives on speed at the till and accuracy behind it: queues move only if the till never stops, and margins hold only if every branch's stock and every cashier's cash add up. Edel does both.

Questions

What happens to the till when the internet goes down?

The till keeps selling. Each sale waits in a queue and is registered as soon as the connection is back, so customers never wait.

Can it manage several branches?

Yes. Each branch has its own stock, tills and cashiers, stock can move between branches, and the owner sees every branch on one dashboard.

Does it handle weighed items?

Yes. Items sold by the kilo are priced by weight, to the gram, and stock is kept in kilos.

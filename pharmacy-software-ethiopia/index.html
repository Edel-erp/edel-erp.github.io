What Edel does for pharmacies

In a pharmacy, money is lost on the shelf: boxes that expire unnoticed, the newest batch sold before the oldest, and stock that never matches the books. Edel tracks every box by batch and expiry from delivery to sale.

  1. Batch and expiry trackingEvery box belongs to a batch with its expiry date, from delivery to sale.
  2. Sell the oldest firstThe till picks the batch that expires first and warns before it is too late.
  3. Expiry alertsSee what expires in 30, 60 and 90 days and its value, in time to act.
  4. Prescription checkPrescription-only items ask the pharmacist to confirm before payment.
  5. Fast dispensingSearch or scan, pick the batch, take payment and print the registered e-invoice.
  6. Clinic credit accountsSell to clinics and companies on credit, with balances and statements.
  7. Supplier price comparisonCompare suppliers' prices and reorder what is running low.
  8. Returns and stock countsReturn near-expiry stock to suppliers and count stock with the loss in Birr.

Questions

Can Edel track expiry dates by batch?

Yes. Every delivery is received as a batch with its expiry date, and stock is tracked per batch until it is sold, returned or written off.

Does the till sell the oldest stock first?

Yes. When you sell a product, Edel picks the batch that expires first and shows its expiry date, so older stock leaves the shelf before newer stock.

Can we sell to clinics on credit?

Yes. Clinics and companies get credit accounts with balances and statements, and each sale is still a registered e-invoice.

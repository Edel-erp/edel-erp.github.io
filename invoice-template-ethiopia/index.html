Questions

Is this invoice valid for the Ministry of Revenues?

It is a plain invoice. Businesses that must use electronic invoicing need registered e-invoices from an approved system, which carry an official number and QR code. Edel issues those.

What should an invoice in Ethiopia show?

At least the seller's name, address and TIN, the buyer's name (and TIN for business customers), an invoice number, the date, each item with quantity and price, and the VAT if you are VAT-registered.

How do I save it as a PDF?

Press 'Print or save as PDF', then choose 'Save as PDF' as the printer. You can then send it by email, WhatsApp or Telegram.

What Edel does for tours & travel

A tour company can be busy every day and still not know which car or route makes money. Edel puts bookings, dispatch, fuel and driver per diem together, so revenue and cost meet on every trip.

  1. Tour bookingsTours, airport shuttles and company trips with route, passengers, guide and agreed rate.
  2. Deposits and creditTake a deposit, confirm with payment, or put an agency or company on credit.
  3. Dispatch boardAssign the car and driver, then record km out and km back for every trip.
  4. Fuel barrelsEvery litre drawn for each car, so fuel used per trip is known.
  5. Profit per car, driver and routeRevenue, fuel, repairs and per diem for each, with cost per km.
  6. Driver per diemApproved by the manager, and counted as a cost only once it is actually paid.
  7. Papers and alertsRegistration and insurance expiry reminders, and an alert when a car is out too long.
  8. Owner dashboardRevenue, costs, profit and trips against last week, from your phone anywhere.

Questions

Can I see profit per car?

Yes. Revenue from each trip and the fuel, repairs and per diem for that car come together, with cost per kilometre.

Does it handle airport shuttles as well as tours?

Yes. Tours, airport shuttles and company use are all booking types, each with its own reporting.

Will I know when a car's papers expire?

Yes. Registration and insurance dates are tracked, with reminders before they expire.

What Edel does for beer distribution

In distribution, profit disappears quietly: a few crates short on a truck, empties left with customers, cash that stays with a seller for days. Edel follows the beer from the brewery to the outlet and the money back to the bank, so the owner can see the gap the same day.

  1. Daily sales per truckEach truck's load, sales, returns and cash recorded every day.
  2. Bottles and cratesKnow how many empties sit with each customer, truck and store.
  3. Seller cash and depositsSee what each seller owes and when the cash reached the bank.
  4. Brewery purchases and targetsTrack orders, credits, and monthly and quarterly brewery targets.
  5. Warehouse stock and countsReceive from the brewery, load trucks and count stock, with losses in Birr.
  6. Bank matchingImport bank statements and match every deposit and payment.
  7. Purchase taxes worked outEthiopian withholding and purchase taxes calculated on every purchase.
  8. Owner dashboardProfit, stock losses, targets and an outlet map on one screen.

Questions

Can Edel track empty crates and bottles?

Yes. Empties going out with each sale and coming back are recorded per customer, truck and store, so you always know how many are outside.

How do I know each seller handed in the cash?

Each truck's sales and the seller's bank deposits are recorded, and the owner sees what each seller still holds.

Does it follow brewery targets?

Yes. Purchases from the brewery are tracked against monthly and quarterly targets, with progress on the owner's dashboard.

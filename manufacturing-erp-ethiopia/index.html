What Edel does for manufacturing

Factories often find out about losses at the end of the month, when stock doesn't match. Edel records each production run as it happens: what went in, what came out, what was scrapped and how long the line stood still.

  1. Recipes and bills of materialsSet exactly what goes into each product, so every batch uses the right amounts.
  2. Production ordersPlan and track each run from raw material to finished goods, including stoppages.
  3. Raw material stockSee what you hold, what each run used, and when imported inputs need reordering.
  4. True cost per productMaterials, labour, power, waste and excise in the cost of every unit.
  5. Waste and scrapRecord losses and rejects per run, and see where material disappears.
  6. Batch tracingTrace any product back to its raw material lots, and recall only what's affected.
  7. Quality checksTest materials and products at set points, and block failed lots from sale.
  8. Machines and power cutsLog servicing, breakdowns and power-cut downtime, so plans match reality.

Questions

Can Edel tell me my cost per unit?

Yes. Each product's recipe adds up materials and packaging, and labour, power and overhead are added on top, so you see the true cost of one unit.

Can it record power cuts?

Yes. Downtime is logged per line, split into power cuts and breakdowns, so production plans reflect what the factory can really make.

Can I trace a batch back to its raw materials?

Yes. Each finished batch keeps the lots of raw material it used, so a problem can be traced and only the affected batches recalled.

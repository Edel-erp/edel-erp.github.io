What Edel does for farms

On a farm, costs are spread over months and fields, and harvest-time crews change every day. Edel charges every input and every day of labour to the block that used it, so the owner sees cost and yield per hectare, not just a total.

  1. Plot and herd recordsEvery field, block, animal and flock on file, with its size, location and history.
  2. Crops and harvestsFollow each planting from sowing to harvest, with yield per field and season.
  3. Inputs and their costSeed, fertiliser, chemicals and feed charged to the field or herd that used them.
  4. Seasonal workersDaily attendance and pay for casual and harvest crews.
  5. Cost per hectare or animalSee what each hectare, animal or batch cost and earned.
  6. Tractors, pumps and trucksFuel, repairs and service dates against the work each machine did.
  7. Sales and exportSales to buyers, the commodity exchange and exporters, with stock and payments linked.
  8. TraceabilityTrace each lot back to its plot, and keep buyer and certification records ready.

Questions

Can I see cost per hectare?

Yes. Inputs and labour are charged to the block that used them, so each block shows its cost, yield and cost per hectare.

Can it pay seasonal workers?

Yes. Crews are marked present each day, assigned to a block, and paid in cash, telebirr or by bank.

Can buyers trace a lot back to the farm?

Yes. Each lot keeps the block, crop and harvest date it came from, ready for buyers and certification.

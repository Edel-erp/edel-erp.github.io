What's changing

Invoices now go through the Ministry of Revenues.

Ethiopia is moving to electronic invoicing. Each sale is sent to the Ministry's system as it happens, and the invoice comes back with an official number and a QR code anyone can scan to check it.

You make a sale

At the till, in the office or on the road. Edel builds the invoice with your TIN, items and VAT.

The Ministry registers it

Edel sends the invoice securely and receives an official invoice number in seconds.

Your customer gets proof

Print it, send it by SMS or email. The QR code lets anyone confirm it's genuine.

Large and VAT-registered taxpayers are expected to move first. Others follow in phases.
Only systems approved by the Ministry can register invoices on your behalf.
When the rules change, Edel updates centrally. You don't reinstall anything.

        FAQ
        
Common questions

      

      

        
How does the waitlist work?
Register once with your business details. We invite businesses in the order they join, with VAT-registered taxpayers first, and call you to arrange setup when your place comes up. Joining is free and you can update your details at any time.

        
Do I need to buy a new machine?
No. Edel runs in a web browser on the computers, tablets and phones you already have. You can connect your existing receipt printer.

        
When does my business have to switch?
The Ministry of Revenues is rolling out electronic invoicing in phases, starting with larger and VAT-registered taxpayers. Take the readiness check above and we'll let you know when your phase is announced.

        
What happens if the internet goes down?
You can keep issuing invoices. Edel stores them safely and registers them with the Ministry as soon as the connection returns.

        
Can I move my data from Excel or another system?
Yes. We import your items, customers, suppliers and opening balances as part of setup, at no extra cost.

        
How is Edel priced?
Monthly or yearly plans in Birr, based on the number of branches and modules. Book a demo and we'll send a quote for your business.

What Edel does for hotels

Hotels lose money in the gaps: a room checked out but not billed, drinks served but not rung up, stock issued to the kitchen with no record. Edel closes those gaps by putting the front desk, the restaurant till and every store on the same system.

  1. Rooms and front deskBookings, check-in, check-out and room status on one screen.
  2. Online bookingsRooms and rates stay in sync with booking sites and your own website.
  3. Online paymentGuests pay by telebirr, CBE Birr, card or bank transfer.
  4. Restaurant and barWaiter orders go straight to the kitchen screen; bills can go to the guest's room.
  5. Kitchen, bar and housekeeping storesEach department requests stock and records exactly what it used.
  6. Daily reportingSales, guests, purchases and cash for every day, matched to your fiscal Z-reports.
  7. Guest QR in every roomGuests register themselves and message the hotel from their phone.
  8. Staff and payrollFingerprint attendance and Ethiopian payroll, ready to send to the bank.

Questions

Can Edel handle both the hotel and its restaurant?

Yes. Rooms, the restaurant and bar till, and the stores are one system, so a meal can be charged to a room and every sale shows in the same daily report.

Does it work with booking sites?

Yes. Room availability and rates can be kept in sync with booking sites and with your own website, so you don't sell the same room twice.

Can I see my hotel's numbers when I'm away?

Yes. The owner sees income, profit, rooms tonight and money received on the phone, every day, from anywhere.
